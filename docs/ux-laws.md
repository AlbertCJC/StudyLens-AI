# 20 UX laws — StudyLens review checklist

Color rule: **60-30-10**. Slate base (60), indigo-tint structure (30), indigo→emerald gradient for the single focus action (10).

| # | Law | How it applies in StudyLens |
|---|-----|-----------------------------|
| 1 | Jakob's Law | Keep familiar patterns: left sidebar, top header, cards, standard form layouts. |
| 2 | Fitts's Law | Primary actions are large (≥44px) and sit where the eye lands: page header right, end of forms. |
| 3 | Hick's Law | Group nav items by task; one primary CTA per view; hide rare actions in menus. |
| 4 | Miller's Law | Chunk dashboards into ≤3–4 metric cards and short lists; paginate long tables. |
| 5 | Postel's Law | Forms accept loose input (trim spaces, case-insensitive email) and give clear inline errors. |
| 6 | Peak-End Rule | Celebrate quiz completion and end of study session; finish flows on a clear success state. |
| 7 | Aesthetic-Usability | Consistent spacing, radius and tokens make the app feel trustworthy and easier. |
| 8 | Von Restorff | Only the focus action gets the gradient, so it stands out. |
| 9 | Tesler's Law | Absorb complexity in the system: sensible defaults, AI summaries, auto-saved progress. |
| 10 | Doherty Threshold | Respond within 400ms: skeletons, optimistic toggles, button loading states. |
| 11 | Zeigarnik Effect | Show unfinished work: "continue where you left off", incomplete quizzes. |
| 12 | Goal-Gradient | Gradient progress bars that visibly approach completion. |
| 13 | Serial Position | Most important nav items first and last; key metric first in rows. |
| 14 | Law of Proximity | Related controls grouped with tight spacing; sections separated by larger gaps. |
| 15 | Common Region | Cards and tinted panels group related content. |
| 16 | Law of Similarity | Same component = same look (one Button, Card, Input) across the app. |
| 17 | Prägnanz | Simple shapes, minimal decoration, clear hierarchy. |
| 18 | Uniform Connectedness | Active nav item and its page header share the structure tint. |
| 19 | Occam's Razor | Remove duplicate pages, redundant borders and decorative noise. |
| 20 | Pareto Principle | Polish the 20% of screens used most first: dashboards, subjects, assessments, flashcards. |
